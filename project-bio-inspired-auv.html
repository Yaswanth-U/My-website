Project 01

Bio-inspired AUV

GUJCOST Robofest Control systems Autonomous navigation Bio inspired design

Overview

Bio inspired Autonomous Underwater Vehicle(AUV) is a project to devlop an AUV that mimics the swimming mechanics of Manta rays. The AUV is designed to navigate underwater environments using a propulsion system that replicates the undulating motion of manta ray fins. The project involves the integration of advanced control systems, sensors, and lightweight materials to create a functional prototype capable of stable and efficient underwater movement. Through iterative design and testing.

Primary objective of the project is to design and devlop a functional AUV prototype.

Technical Approach

The initial aim of project is to win GUJCOST Robofest competition, so the design and development of AUV is focused on the requirements and constraints of the competition. The project will involve a multidisciplinary approach, combining principles of mechanical engineering, control systems, and robotics to create a functional AUV prototype. The design process will include iterative prototyping and testing to optimize the performance and stability of the AUV in underwater environments.

all of the contents with in this project are open sourced and available in github check it out if you're intersted.

Current Status: The project is currently in ideation phase, and yet to be devloped.

Outcomes & Learnings

The project is expected to contribute to the field of bio-inspired robotics and advance our understanding of underwater locomotion. By mimicking the swimming mechanics of manta rays, the AUV has the potential to achieve efficient and stable movement in underwater environments, which could have applications in marine research, environmental monitoring, and underwater exploration. Additionally, the project will provide valuable insights into the design and control of bio-inspired robotic systems, paving the way for future innovations in underwater robotics.
